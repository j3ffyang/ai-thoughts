# Ground Truth and Growth

## Overview
A personal reflection on how working with AI agents teaches patience, clarity, and the value of ground truth through terminal-based computing, culminating in an iterative workflow loop.

## Learning Objectives
The viewer will understand:
1. What "ground truth" means in the context of terminal computing and AI collaboration
2. The key lessons learned from working with AI agents (pace, patience, clarity)
3. The iterative loop workflow that makes AI collaboration effective
