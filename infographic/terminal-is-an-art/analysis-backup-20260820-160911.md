## Main Topic
A personal essay arguing that the Unix/Linux terminal is the closest thing to an art form in computing — spanning simplicity, directness, honesty, power, workflow, universality, and the deep understanding it provides.

## Learning Objectives
After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. The seven core qualities that make the terminal exceptional (Simple, Direct, Honest, Powerful, Workflow, Universal, Understanding)
2. Why the terminal's design philosophy — unchanged for 50 years — rewards long-term skill investment
3. How terminal commands compose into powerful tools through pipes and transparency

## Target Audience
- **Knowledge Level**: Beginner to Intermediate — tech-curious readers and developers
- **Context**: Personal essay / blog post — readers arrive for opinion, stay for argument
- **Expectations**: Understand why someone would prefer a terminal over modern GUIs
