## Main Topic
A personal 35-year journey with Unix/Linux: from discovering AIX at IBM in the early 90s, through being hacked and forming a lifelong security habit, the distro years ending at Arch Linux, and finally wiping all Windows from his machines.

## Learning Objectives
After viewing this infographic, the viewer will understand:
1. How one person's 35-year path ran AIX → Fedora/Ubuntu/Debian/Manjaro → Arch, told as a milestone journey.
2. That being hacked turned security (encryption, ssh, firewalls) into a permanent habit that paid off in the cloud era.
3. The recurring themes behind his setup: minimalism, no bloatware, open-source, and eventually "all machines are Arch Linux".
