## Main Topic
First-person experience essay on how to customize an AI agent while working — writing your own SKILL.md (procedure), AGENTS.md (repo rules), and PERSONA.md (personality) instead of hunting for ready-made skills, with a 5-step loop and the benefits of ownership.

## Learning Objectives
After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. Why writing your own skill/config files beats searching skill marketplaces: the result does exactly what you want because you wrote it.
2. The three customization levels — SKILL.md, AGENTS.md, PERSONA.md — and how each is used on the fly.
3. The 5-step on-the-fly loop (spot → write → use → tighten → own) and the benefits (simple, secure, independent, accurate, smarter).
