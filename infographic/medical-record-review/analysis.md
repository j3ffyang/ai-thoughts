## Main Topic

A two-layer skill for AI-assisted review of personal medical records. A generic procedure carries no patient data; a project layer injects configuration and facts. The value is discipline — refusing to guess — not cleverer analysis. The evidence discipline transfers; the clinical judgement does not.

## Learning Objectives

After viewing this infographic, the viewer should understand:
1. Why a procedure beats a better prompt for messy personal health records.
2. The three-layer authority chain and why the split is what makes the skill publishable.
3. The ten rules that carry the weight, and the boundary of what the skill refuses to do.

## Target Audience

- **Knowledge Level**: Intermediate — people who already use AI agents and skills.
- **Context**: Describes a working skill; readers evaluate whether to adopt it.
- **Expectations**: The why, the rules, and where the limits are.

- **Data Structure**: A hierarchy (AGENTS.md > project layer > generic skill), a flat rule set of ten, and a linear workflow of four phases.
