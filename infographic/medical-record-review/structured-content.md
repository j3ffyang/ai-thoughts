# Health Records an Agent Can Audit

## Overview

A two-layer skill for reviewing personal health records with an AI agent. The procedure refuses to guess; the facts stay local; nothing in the output claims clinical authority.

## Learning Objectives

The viewer will understand:
1. Why a procedure beats a better prompt for messy personal health records.
2. The three-layer authority chain and why the split makes the skill publishable.
3. The ten rules that carry the weight, and where the skill's authority stops.

## Design Instructions

### Style Preferences
- Default craft-handmade: warm pastels, cream paper, hand-drawn lines, paper-cut shadows
- No photographic or realistic imagery

### Layout Preferences
- bento-grid, landscape 16:9
- Hero cell dominant; ten-rule chip grid as the densest cell; footer disclaimer separated

### Other Requirements
- English throughout
- No patient data, no numbers that could identify a person
- Every string rendered exactly once
