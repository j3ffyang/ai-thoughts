# Unix Is Art

## Overview

A bento-grid visual summary of the Unix philosophy as an aesthetic: the thesis, the timeline from 1969 to 2004, the core principles, the critics, a remembrance of Dennis Ritchie, and the author's personal take.

## Learning Objectives

The viewer will understand:

1. What the Unix philosophy is and its timeline.
2. Its core principles.
3. Why it reads as art — the beauty is in how the parts fit.
