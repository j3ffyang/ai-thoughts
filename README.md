# ai_thoughts

[中文版](README_zh.md)

A bilingual (English · 中文) collection of articles and essays spanning three domains: **technology** (hands-on experience with OpenClaw 🦞 and Hermes Agent ⚕, privacy, knowledge management), **history** (Five Dynasties & Ten Kingdoms, silk, *Dream of the Red Chamber*), and **culture & philosophy** (motorcycle culture, how different cultures face death, the "unknown unknowns" of knowledge). Topics range from practical how-tos (building custom skills, hardening a self-hosted deployment) to more personal writing (solo entrepreneurship, riding culture).

Images for each article live in the [`imgs/`](imgs/) subdirectory and follow the same `YYMMDDHHММ_slug` naming convention as the articles themselves.
