### Rolling Block Rifle Glitch Steps

1. Complete legendary grizzly hunt with Hosea, follow him back to camp
2. Do NOT enter camp - wait for Hosea to dismount Silver Dollar and finish conversation
3. Ride own horse close to Silver Dollar, jump on it
4. Ride Silver Dollar far from camp so it won't walk back
5. Bomb Silver Dollar to kill it and drop the Rolling Block Rifle

Note: you'll need this rifle for a 660 ft long-scope kill

### Strange Statues Puzzle (3 gold bars)

**Location:** Cave east of Donner Falls, beneath the "I" in AMBARINO on map.

**Clue (optional):** Strange Painting at Window Rock (NW of Fort Wallace).

**Solution:**
1. Enter cave, count fingers on each of the 7 statues (statue missing an arm counts as 7 - the arm lies on the floor nearby)
2. Press front buttons on statues with prime number fingers: **2, 3, 5, 7**
3. Harpy statue opens → take 3 gold bars
4. To reset: press rear button on any statue

### Jack Hall Gang Treasure (2 gold bars)

**Start:** Buy map 1 from Maximo near Flatneck Station (\$10), or find/kill him.

**Map 1 → Caliban's Seat** (south of Valentine): scale east side, follow path through rocks, find second map.

**Map 2 → Cotorra Springs:** west end, central stack of rocks → money and third map. (Beware: Legendary Wolf lurks here.)

**Map 3 → O'Creagh's Run** (north of Emerald Ranch): small rocky island in middle of lake, under a rock → **2 gold bars**.

### Poisonous Trail Treasure (4 gold bars)

**Start:** Poisonous Trail Map 1 found in a lockbox under the bed inside the Cairn Lake cabin (same spot as Flaco Hernandez mission). Beware: Willard's Rest nearby.

**Map 1 → Face Rock** (Scarlett Meadows, west of Ringneck Creek): map in a hollow tree at the base of the face-shaped rock → get Map 2.

**Map 2 → Snake Geoglyph** (west of Van Horn): map under a pile of rocks in the snake's coiled center → get Map 3.

**Map 3 → Elysian Pool waterfall** (Roanoke Ridge): cave behind the falls, navigate the cavern (lantern required, past the abandoned campsite lockbox with a jewel) → **4 gold bars** on the ledge. ($500 each at any fence.)

*Acquired — 4 gold bars collected.*

## Store Backroom (Illicit) Business Robberies

Tip received: some stores at Strawberry are illicit — check for strange activity.

There are 4 backroom businesses (General Store, Gunsmith, Doctor's office). How to rob, same pattern (PC keyboard): confirm the tip, enter the store, aim weapon at the shopkeep (**right mouse button**), the "Rob Back Room" option appears — be close and stay out of sight. **Important: the option only appears AFTER you become "aware" of the illicit business** — peer through the covered rear window and observe the lawman taking a bribe from the back door / knock on the rear security door, or receive a tip from an NPC (e.g. Tilly at camp) / freed prisoner.

- ~~**Rhodes Gunsmith**~~ - Lancaster Repeater in a weapon case opposite the bottom of the stairs. See Weapon Strategy (acquired)
- ~~**Strawberry General Store**~~ - flip on ("you can now rob the side business") by peering through the basement window beneath the walkway; ~\$50 cash + supplies in the basement. No Wanted level if you don't draw attention — the shopkeep can't report it (his business is illegal). Don't loot the store shelves on the way out while someone's watching (acquired)
- **Valentine Doctor's office** - Schofield Revolver + cash + locked safe. Always draws the law (Sheriff's office next door): rob the register, have him open the back, Dead Eye the O'Driscolls inside, grab the Schofield + cash, then run. Lowest bounty ~$5.
- **Saint Denis Gunsmith** - high-stakes secret in the garden behind the shop

## Herbalist Challenge

**Herbalist 2: Pick and Eat 4 Species of Berry** (reward: $5, Health EXP)

Must pick and eat one of each of the 4 berry species. The only 4 in the game.

**Important: you must select the "Eat" option directly from the bush on the ground.** Picking them and eating from the satchel does NOT count.

- ~~Red Raspberry~~ (eaten) — most common, found nearly everywhere (plains, e.g. Scarlett Meadows); also fields west of Valentine. Most of The Heartlands
- **Wintergreen Berry** — hardest to spot: tiny, low-profile bush (red berries), masked by foliage. Use Eagle Eye. Reliable spots:
  - **Cumberland Forest (east end)**: a bit north of the "T" in Cumberland Forest on the map, in open forest floor — **confirmed pair found here** (one just north of the "T", the second right beside it around the berry icon on the map)
  - **Big Valley (westernmost path)**: west of the "B" in "Big Valley" on the map — at the intersection of two fallen trees west of the main path (right near a cougar spawn), and further north in a small clearing just off the main path
  - **NNW of Owanjila Lake**: follow the road north from Owanjila, duck off the left side just as the road and river separate (cougar/grizzly territory — keep a weapon ready)
  - **NE New Hanover/Ambarino**: north of Annesburg behind Manito Glade (Hermit Stranger location), near the legendary moose spawn (Brandywine Drop), near Doverhill
  - Also reportedly near the legendary bear location, O'Creagh's Run (Grizzlies East)
- ~~**Evergreen Huckleberry**~~ (eaten) — near the Kamassa River, both Lemoyne & New Hanover (e.g. NW of Saint Denis / Bayou Nwa; the swamps); watch for gators & ambushes there
- **Blackberry** — bushes are large, easy to spot from horseback; the entire bush lights up under Eagle Eye. Reliable spots:
  - **Big Valley, NW of the "B" in "Big Valley" on the map** — a pair of bushes near a Lady Slipper Orchid location (best bet, confirmed by IGN)
  - **NW of Strawberry** — just slightly off the road (Shacknews); predators in the area — keep a weapon ready
  - **Along the Dakota River banks** (compendium lists West Elizabeth + Dakota River as its favored habitat)
  - **Lucky's Cabin (Heartlands)** — blackberries also grow here alongside Indian Tobacco, Bay Bolete, Creeping Thyme, Oregano

Quick plan: Raspberry (~Heartlands/Scarlett) → Blackberry (Big Valley) → Evergreen Huckleberry (Kamassa River / Bayou Nwa) → Wintergreen (Cumberland Forest) — each is a different direction from camp, so one berry per trip unless you route them.

## Sharpshooter Challenge (7 Consecutive Headshots)

Sharpshooter 7 — get 7 headshots in a row (reward: Sharpshooter Off-Hand Holster). The hardest challenge so far: the counter resets to 0 on any non-headshot kill.

- **O'Driscoll camp, Six Point Cabin (Cumberland Forest, NE of Valentine)** — raided 4 times; killed/lassoed at least 7 and dragged them a short distance. Too time-consuming, never completed.
- **Train robbery** — 4 times. Buy a ticket to Riggs Station, wear a mask, carry a repeater + pistol with enough ammo, and kill all the lawmen (they keep coming even with the train stopped on the high bridge). Succeeded once — 7 consecutive headshots on the passengers — but the white Arabian jumped off the high bridge and died. Couldn't live without my white Arabian horse, so I had to restart — losing the achievement. Never got another chance; progress always read 0.
- **Alligators (peaceful route)** — alligator headshots count too. Lagras and north of Saint Denis: killed 5, could not find a 6th. Then the pond in Bayou Nwa (north of Saint Denis / west of Lagras): found 6+ alligators and headshot them, then killed a boar eating a dead alligator.
- **Completed.** Would not want to play this one again — 5 in a row would be plenty and far less punishing.

## Serial Killer "American Dreams"

Stranger quest — find 3 murder scenes, each has a severed head holding a **map piece**. The map pieces reveal the killer's location; capture him alive and deliver to the **sheriff in Valentine**.

- **Under train tracks south of Valentine** (found) — corpse cut in half and hung from the bridge over the track gap; head on a support beam to the side, map piece in mouth
- **Scarlett Meadows, south of Rhodes** — body at the "S" of "MEADOWS" on the map; head on a pike behind the tree (easy to spot at night — lantern at the base of the hanging tree)
- **Big Valley, south of Wallace Station** — follow Little Creek River west from the Dakota River; body on a larger rock, head with the map on a smaller rock nearby

Reward: when Lowry attacks Sheriff Malloy in the Valentine cell, kill him with Dead Eye to save Malloy → $20 + honor (varies slightly with honor). Don't kill him in the cabin fight — mission fails.
