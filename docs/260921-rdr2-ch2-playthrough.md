## Weapon Strategy & Acquisitions

- Collect them freely, patiently, and have fun
- Never purchase at gunsmith, unless it's free
- **Important for a no-Chapter-3 run** — this section is effectively a must: several weapons (Bolt Action, Rolling Block) aren't obtainable cheaply later, so get them in Chapter 2. The Rolling Block Rifle glitch below is the free way to get it early.

| Weapon | Source | Method |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Rolling Block Rifle | Hosea | Glitch |
| Lancaster Repeater | Rhodes gunsmith | Robbery after releasing boy in basement |
| Bolt Action Rifle | Sheriff Malloy, Valentine | Wait for the public hanging event; Malloy carries it on his shoulder. After the hanging ends, follow him away from the gallows, then murder him and pick up the rifle. ~\$20 bounty. If you miss it, a bounty hunter in "The First Shall Be Last" (Sean's rescue, Chapter 2) drops one — after the stealth kills, shoot the left bounty hunter in the back first (before he notices) |
| Schofield Revolver | Valentine Doctor's office backroom | Looted during the backroom robbery — see Store Backroom (Illicit) Business Robberies |
| Flaco's Revolver | Flaco Hernandez, Cairn Lake cabin (Grizzlies West) | Duel in "The Noblest of Men, and a Woman" side quest; loot his body after. Rare, cannot be customized. Loot the cabin lockbox under the bed for the Poisonous Trail map 1 (see Gold Bars) |

### Rolling Block Rifle Glitch Steps

1. Complete legendary grizzly hunt with Hosea, follow him back to camp
2. Do NOT enter camp - wait for Hosea to dismount Silver Dollar and finish conversation
3. Ride own horse close to Silver Dollar, jump on it
4. Ride Silver Dollar far from camp so it won't walk back
5. Bomb Silver Dollar to kill it and drop the Rolling Block Rifle

Note: the only way to get the Rolling Block Rifle in Chapter 2 — miss it and it won't be available until later chapters. Essential for the 660 ft long-scope kill (Sharpshooter 6).

## Store Backroom (Illicit) Business Robberies

Tip received: some stores at Strawberry are illicit — check for strange activity.

There are 4 backroom businesses (General Store, Gunsmith, Doctor's office). How to rob, same pattern (PC keyboard): confirm the tip, enter the store, aim weapon at the shopkeep (**right mouse button**), the "Rob Back Room" option appears — be close and stay out of sight. **Important: the option only appears AFTER you become "aware" of the illicit business** — peer through the covered rear window and observe the lawman taking a bribe from the back door / knock on the rear security door, or receive a tip from an NPC (e.g. Tilly at camp) / freed prisoner.

| Store | Loot | How |
|-------|------|-----|
| Rhodes Gunsmith | Lancaster Repeater | Weapon case opposite the bottom of the stairs (see Weapon Strategy) |
| Strawberry General Store | ~\$50 cash + supplies | Flip on ("you can now rob the side business") by peering through the basement window beneath the walkway. No Wanted level if you don't draw attention — the shopkeep can't report it (his business is illegal). Don't loot the store shelves on the way out while someone's watching |
| Valentine Doctor's office | Schofield Revolver + cash + locked safe | Always draws the law (Sheriff's office next door): rob the register, have him open the back, Dead Eye the O'Driscolls inside, grab the Schofield + cash, then run. Lowest bounty ~\$5 |
| Saint Denis Gunsmith | Garden secret | High-stakes secret in the garden behind the shop |

## Trinket Components

All Chapter-2 fence crafts are done — trinkets: Beaver Tooth, Buck Antler, Coyote Fang, Elk Antler, Fox Claw, Moose Antler, Panther's Eye, Ram Horn, Wolf Heart; talismans: Bear Claw, Boar Tusk, Bison Horn. Legendary keepsakes are crafted at a **Fence**, while pelts and carcasses go to the **Trapper** — different vendors; the keepsake is consumed by the craft, the pelt stays usable for clothing. Still out of reach in Chapter 2: Cougar Fang, Pronghorn Horn and Tatanka Bison Horn (New Austin / Epilogue) and the Alligator Tooth Talisman (Bullgator, Chapter 4).

## Herbalist Challenge

Herbalist 2 — pick and eat 1 of each of the 4 berry species. **Eat directly from the bush** (eating from the satchel doesn't count).

| Berry | Location |
|-------|----------|
| Red Raspberry | Common everywhere (Scarlett Meadows, Heartlands) |
| Evergreen Huckleberry | Kamassa River (Lemoyne / New Hanover swamps) |
| Wintergreen Berry | Tiny bush, use Eagle Eye — Cumberland Forest (north of the "T"), or Big Valley / NNW of Owanjila |
| Blackberry | Big Valley (NW of the "B"), or NW of Strawberry — large bush, easy from horseback |

Quick plan: Raspberry (~Heartlands/Scarlett) → Blackberry (Big Valley) → Evergreen Huckleberry (Kamassa River / Bayou Nwa) → Wintergreen (Cumberland Forest) — each is a different direction from camp, so one berry per trip unless you route them.

## Sharpshooter Challenge

### 7 Consecutive Headshots

Sharpshooter 7 — get 7 headshots in a row (reward: Sharpshooter Off-Hand Holster). The hardest challenge so far — the counter resets to 0 on any non-headshot kill.

**Alligators (peaceful route) — the method that works:** alligator headshots count too. Lagras and north of Saint Denis: killed 5, could not find a 6th. The pond in Bayou Nwa (north of Saint Denis / west of Lagras) has 6+ alligators — headshot them, then a boar eating a dead alligator. **Completed** — would not play this one again; 5 in a row would be plenty and far less punishing.

**Failed attempts (not recommended):**

- **O'Driscoll camp, Six Point Cabin (Cumberland Forest, NE of Valentine)** — raided 4 times; killed/lassoed at least 7 and dragged them a short distance. Too time-consuming, never completed.
- **Train robbery** — 4 times. Buy a ticket to Riggs Station, wear a mask, carry a repeater + pistol with enough ammo, and kill all the lawmen (they keep coming even with the train stopped on the high bridge). Succeeded once — 7 consecutive headshots on the passengers — but the white Arabian jumped off the high bridge and died. Couldn't live without my white Arabian horse, so I had to restart — losing the achievement. Never got another chance; progress always read 0.

## Master Hunter Challenge

Ranks 1–10 completed in Chapter 2 — full set earned along the way (Off-Hand Holster → Bandolier → Gun Belt → Holster). Ranks run strictly in order; work done before a rank is active does not count.

| Rank | Objective | Reward |
|------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Skin 3 deer | Master Hunter Off-Hand Holster |
| 2 | 3 perfect rabbit pelts | \$5 + Health XP |
| 3 | Track 10 different species with binoculars | Master Hunter Bandolier |
| 4 | Call an animal, then 5 clean kills | \$10 + Health XP |
| 5 | Skin 3 black or grizzly bears | \$10 + Health XP |
| 6 | Kill 5 cougars with a bow, then skin them | \$15 + Health XP |
| 7 | Bait and kill a herbivore and a predator | Master Hunter Gun Belt |
| 8 | Catch 3 small fish without a fishing rod | \$15 + Health XP |
| 9 | Kill an opossum playing possum | \$20 + Health XP — unlocks Giaguaro |
| 10 | Kill the legendary panther Giaguaro | Master Hunter Holster |

Notes: rabbit pelts want a clean Varmint Rifle headshot. Rank 6 is the grind — 5 cougars, bow only, so bait and high ground help. Rank 8 fish can be shot and picked up; the Granite Pass waterfall drops them on the bank. Rank 9 opossums spawn at night — spook one until it plays dead, then shoot it. Rank 10 is the only legendary animal that is challenge-gated rather than chapter-gated, so it is reachable inside Chapter 2 (see Legendary Animal Pelts). Giaguaro is aggressive: save manually before entering the territory, track 3 clues to the carcass pile, and expect it to charge — Dead Eye + a repeater/shotgun, it can kill in one hit.

## Serial Killer "American Dreams"

Stranger quest — find 3 murder scenes, each has a severed head holding a **map piece**. The map pieces reveal the killer's location; capture him alive and deliver to the **sheriff in Valentine**.

- **Under train tracks south of Valentine** — corpse cut in half and hung from the bridge over the track gap; head on a support beam to the side, map piece in mouth
- **Scarlett Meadows, south of Rhodes** — body at the "S" of "MEADOWS" on the map; head on a pike behind the tree (easy to spot at night — lantern at the base of the hanging tree)
- **Big Valley, south of Wallace Station** — follow Little Creek River west from the Dakota River; body on a larger rock, head with the map on a smaller rock nearby

Reward: when Lowry attacks Sheriff Malloy in the Valentine cell, kill him with Dead Eye to save Malloy → $20 + honor (varies slightly with honor). Don't kill him in the cabin fight — mission fails.
