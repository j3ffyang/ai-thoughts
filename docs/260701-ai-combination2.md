# Optimizing AI Usage: From Doubao User to Power User

## Introduction
- Overview of evolving AI usage for IT professionals.
- Challenges in daily AI integration for documents and presentations.

## Challenges
- **Dependence** on a single AI provider limits flexibility.
- High costs and subscription fees for **multiple platforms**.
- **Rapid consumption** of usage tokens.
- Risk of access **bans**.

## Solutions: AI Usage Combination Strategy

### OpenRouter.ai
- Simplify payments with a **single billing platform**.
- Pay based on **actual usage**, avoiding costly subscriptions.
- Access to **multiple high-quality free AI models** (e.g., Nvidia Nemotron).

> Limitation: some free AI models are very slow

### OpenCode.ai
- **Manage and switch seamlessly** between 100+ AI providers.
- Efficient **command-line interface** for rapid switching.

> Limitation: command line _only_

### Enhancing Efficiency with AI Agents
- Use AI agents like **Hermes and OpenClaw**.
- Automate **multitasking and complex workflows** using predefined skills (SKILL.md).
- Delegate **repetitive or back-and-forth tasks** to these agents.


## Workflow for Using AI Agents (Hermes and OpenClaw)

1. Start with an **idea** and write it into a **generic skill framework**.
2. Collaborate with AI to **polish and brainstorm** your skill framework, refining it into a **production-level SKILL.md**.
3. **Install** the custom skill in AI agents.
4. **Trigger and execute** the custom skill using the AI agents.

## Benefits
- **Cost efficiency** through usage-based payment models.
- **Increased flexibility** and reduced supplier lock-in.
- **Productivity gains** via AI agent automation.
- **Better control** over AI resource management.

## Conclusion
**Combining platforms and AI agents** empowers more effective AI adoption by solving key challenges:

1. Simplifying to a **single payment** platform.
2. Allowing **flexible switching** between multiple providers and AI models on the fly.
3. Reducing the **risk of being banned** by diversifying AI sources.
4. Enabling creation and execution of **custom AI skills** to automate workflows.
